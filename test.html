I used to think waking up tired was just part of normal life.

For a long time, mornings felt heavy — even on days when I slept "enough." I didn't think much of it. I assumed that's just how adulthood feels.

What surprised me was realizing it wasn't about motivation, discipline, or forcing myself to wake up earlier. It came down to one small habit I was repeating every night without noticing.

The habit was simple: late-night phone use. Scrolling, watching videos, answering messages — right up until sleep.

I didn't quit my phone completely. I just gave myself a short buffer before bed — no screens, low light, and something calming instead.

I'm still experimenting, and results vary for everyone, but mornings now feel noticeably lighter and clearer.

*   *   *

If you want to try this yourself tonight:

  • Put your phone away 30–60 minutes before sleep
  • Lower the lights and slow things down
  • Replace scrolling with something calming (reading, breathing, or a warm drink)

That alone might already help.
